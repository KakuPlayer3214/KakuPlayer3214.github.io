What is bovine spongiform encephalopathy (BSE)?

Bureau of Public Health, Tokyo Metropolitan Government (Tokyo Food Safety Information Center)

Main Arguments

BSE is a fatal neurological cattle disease caused by abnormal prions affecting the central nervous system. It details the spread via infected meat-and-bone meal (MBM) and its public health implications.

Methodology

Review and synthesis of official public health documentation, epidemiological research, and historical data originating from the UK outbreak.

Conclusion

Due to the absence of early detection or treatment, strict control over animal feed production and preventing contaminated MBM from entering the agricultural supply chain are essential.

About Variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease (vCJD)

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) / NCEZID

Main Arguments

Establishes a strong correlation between human vCJD cases and the consumption of BSE-contaminated beef products, highlighting the success of regulatory suppression.

Methodology

Comprehensive analysis of 233 global case reports, epidemiological surveillance data, diagnostic criteria, clinical history, and post-mortem tissue autopsies.

Conclusion

Feed bans and removing specified risk materials (SRMs) successfully suppressed vCJD, making new cases extremely rare since the early 2000s. Preventive policies are paramount.

Nature Scientific Reports URL

Biodegradation of bovine spongiform encephalopathy prions in compost

Shanwei Xu, Sujeema J. Abeysekara, et al.

Main Arguments

Evaluates aerobic composting as a cost-effective alternative to incineration or alkaline hydrolysis for degrading BSE prions in specified risk materials (SRMs).

Methodology

Assessed prion degradation across a 28-day lab-scale system (PMCA assays) and a 106–120-day bin-scale composting system (~2,200 kg) using transgenic mouse bioassays.

Conclusion

Composting reduces prion contamination risk; however, spatial temperature variations prevent it from consistently reaching the regulatory 5 log10 reduction threshold as a standalone disposal method.
